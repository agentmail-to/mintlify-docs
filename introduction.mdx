AgentMail is an API platform that gives AI agents their own email inboxes to send, receive, and act on email.

## What your agent can do with its own inbox

- Sign up for services and read the verification codes and receipts that come back.
- Hold a real identity on [your own domain](/advanced/custom-domains) that teammates can CC and forward to.
- Reach anyone, and be reached, over the protocol everyone already speaks.
- Keep agent mail out of human mailboxes.

## Why AgentMail

- Create an inbox in one call, then [send](/core/send), [receive](/core/receive), and reply from it.
- [Webhooks](/advanced/webhooks) and [WebSockets](/advanced/websockets) wake your agent the moment mail lands.
- [Threads](/core/conversations) arrive assembled, quotes stripped, attachments [extracted to text](/core/receive).
- Viruses and spam are screened out before your agent sees them. Nothing to configure.
- Server-side [allow and block lists](/core/inbound-control) bound who an inbox can mail. [Drafts](/core/send) act as a human-approval gate.
- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass out of the box. A [custom domain](/advanced/custom-domains) gets its DNS records generated.

## Examples

- An [outbound SDR agent](/examples/outbound-gtm-sdr-agent) that researches leads, sends first touches, and follows up.
- A [customer support agent](/examples/customer-support-agent) that answers a support inbox from your docs.
- An [AI employee](/examples/ai-employee-with-its-own-inbox) with an address teammates can CC and forward to.

# Understand what AgentMail is and where to start

AgentMail is an email API built for AI agents. An inbox gives an agent a real address it receives mail at and sends from, both directions driven through the API. Read this page to pick a starting surface, then go to `/quickstart` for the runnable loop.

## Facts

- An AgentMail inbox is a real email address. The agent receives mail at the address and sends from the address through the API.
- The inbox address appears in the From line of the agent's mail and can live on your own domain (see `/advanced/custom-domains`).
- Mail addressed to the agent's inbox stays out of human mailboxes because the address belongs to the agent.
- Allow and block lists decide who an inbox can send to and receive from (see `/core/inbound-control`).
- AgentMail handles the SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication that keeps an inbox's mail deliverable (see `/advanced/deliverability`).
- Inbound mail is screened before the agent sees anything. Viruses are rejected at the gateway. Everything else is labeled on arrival (see `/core/receive`).
- Labels such as `spam` and `unauthenticated` are hidden from the agent's default view.
- When a message lands, a webhook (`/advanced/webhooks`) or WebSocket (`/advanced/websockets`) event wakes the agent.
- The agent reads the whole thread for context (`/core/conversations`), then sends its reply through the same API into the same conversation (`/core/send`).
- `extracted_text` on a message holds only what the sender just wrote, with quoted history stripped. Attachments such as PDFs, Word documents, and spreadsheets return pre-extracted text via `text_url` (see `/core/receive`).
- Errors that reach the API return a stable `code` and a `docs` link, plus a `fix` field stating the corrective action when a generic remedy applies. A taken inbox username returns up to three available alternatives in `suggestions` (see `/advanced/errors`).
- Send endpoints accept an `Idempotency-Key`, so a retried send cannot deliver the same email twice (see `/core/send`).
- Drafts double as a human-approval gate: the agent writes the draft, a human reviews and sends it (see `/core/send`).
- Pods isolate tenants inside an organization, and API keys scope to the organization, one pod, or one inbox (see `/advanced/multi-tenant`).
- Receiving email is free. Plans meter outbound sends (see `/advanced/plans-and-usage`).
- The Quickstart (`/quickstart`) covers getting a key, creating an inbox, and running the full send, receive, and reply loop from the CLI, curl, or the SDKs.
- Integration setup pages exist under `/integrations/frameworks/` for LangChain, OpenAI Agents SDK, Vercel AI SDK, Google ADK, Mastra, eve, LiveKit Agents, Sim, Replit, OpenClaw, Grok, Hermes, Claude Cowork, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, and Devin.
- Any MCP-capable client can connect through MCP and Skills (see `/integrations/mcp-and-skills`).
- The four surfaces: CLI and SDKs (`/integrations/sdks-and-cli`), MCP and Skills (`/integrations/mcp-and-skills`), the Console web dashboard at `https://console.agentmail.to`, and the HTTP API reference (`/api-reference/inboxes/list-inboxes`).
- Support: the AgentMail Discord at `https://discord.gg/hTYatWYWBc`, or email `support@agentmail.cc` for anything you would rather not post publicly.

## Not supported

- Running email flows through AgentMail requires no person watching an email client.
- You do not configure SPF, DKIM, or DMARC yourself. AgentMail handles that authentication.
- A message carrying a virus is rejected at the gateway and does not reach the agent.
- Mail labeled `spam` or `unauthenticated` does not appear in the agent's default view.
